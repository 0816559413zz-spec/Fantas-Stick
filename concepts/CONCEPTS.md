## #2 Cliffside Castle — วาดใหม่เป็นสีน้ำมุมสูง
- สถานะ: 📝 พรอมต์พร้อม • ชนิด: แผนที่ • แท็ก: สีน้ำ, ปราสาท, แมพปัจจุบัน

แมพที่มีอยู่ในเกม (fcs). ใช้ภาพมุมสูงเป็นผัง/ภูมิประเทศ + สไตล์สีน้ำ. งานอาร์ตหลักของเกมเป็นสีน้ำ.

- Ref: `designs/sky-castle_ref/03_current_map_overview.webp`
- Ref: `designs/sky-castle_ref/02_style_watercolor_castle.png`

**พรอมต์:**
```
Use the two attached images. Image 1 is a 3D game-engine overview of a map: use it ONLY for the layout and terrain. Image 2 is the art-style reference.

Repaint Image 1 as a single hand-painted watercolor and ink concept-art illustration, aerial three-quarter view, landscape 16:9.

KEEP THE LAYOUT from Image 1: a grassy hill range with pine forests on the upper-left; a long rocky peninsula jutting into a calm lake, with cliffs on its sides; on the peninsula a walled medieval castle courtyard with a tall gothic castle keep with pointed spires at the far end, a plaza with stone paving, small buildings and a blue-roofed hall near the entrance; a second headland on the right with a small gate tower; two small sea stacks (rocks) in the water; wide open lake in the foreground.

STYLE (from Image 2): traditional watercolor with sepia ink linework, soft translucent washes, paper texture, loose wet-on-wet edges, sandstone and cream castle stone, moss-green and ochre grass, deep teal-blue water with pale brush ripples, soft blue-grey shadows, white paper vignette at the edges. Hand-painted, NOT 3D render, NOT photoreal.

IMPROVE: make the castle keep the clear focal point with warm detailed stone, banners and tiny windows; add a few wizards flying on broomsticks above the courtyard and lake for scale; light mist over the water; a few birds; clouds in a pale sky. Golden-hour light from the upper left.

Avoid: text, logos, watermark, modern objects, UI, red debug overlays, grid lines.
```

## #1 Sky Castle — ปราสาทบนเกาะลอยฟ้า (มองออกจากปราสาท)
- สถานะ: 📝 พรอมต์พร้อม • ชนิด: แผนที่ • แท็ก: สีน้ำ, ปราสาท, ไม้กวาด, แมพถัดไป

มุมมองยืนบนระเบียงปราสาทมองออกไปเห็นเกาะลอยฟ้า หอคอย 3 หลังมีสะพานเชื่อม พ่อมดขี่ไม้กวาด 2 คน (ซ้ายล่างใกล้ / ขวาล่างไกล). สไตล์สีน้ำตาม Ref 02. ตีความสเก็ตช์เอง — แก้ได้.

- Ref: `designs/sky-castle_ref/01_composition_sketch.webp`
- Ref: `designs/sky-castle_ref/02_style_watercolor_castle.png`

**พรอมต์:**
```
Use the two attached images. Image 1 is a rough composition sketch; Image 2 is the art-style reference.

Create a single landscape concept-art illustration (16:9) for a wizard-duel game map: a medieval stone castle on a floating sky island, seen from the castle's own battlement/balcony looking OUT over the sky.

STYLE (from Image 2): traditional watercolor and ink painting, soft translucent washes, visible paper texture, loose wet-on-wet bleeding edges, muted warm sandstone and cream stone, moss green and ochre grass patches, pale blue-grey shadows, thin sepia ink linework, white paper showing through in the highlights, vignette fading into white at the edges. Hand-painted, NOT 3D render, NOT photoreal, NOT cartoon.

COMPOSITION (from Image 1):
- Foreground left-top: a stone battlement ledge with a slender pointed spire, framing the view.
- Centre: three tall round castle towers with crenellated tops and conical slate roofs, linked by an angled stone bridge / covered walkway between them; the curved underside of a floating rock island visible below the left tower, with roots and small hanging vegetation.
- Bottom-left: a wizard in a long dark cloak seen from behind, flying on a broomstick angled diagonally upward to the right, cloak trailing in wind.
- Bottom-right: a second, smaller wizard on a broom, banking downward, further away to show depth.
- Open sky in the lower middle, with distant floating rock islets, soft clouds, a few birds.
- Strong depth: warm detailed foreground, hazy blue-grey atmospheric perspective in the distance.

MOOD: daytime, soft golden-hour light from the upper left, calm and epic, a little whimsical. Clear readable silhouettes of towers, bridge and flying wizards.

Avoid: text, logos, watermark, modern buildings, guns, realistic faces, heavy outlines, photo look.
```
