## Visual reading of the user's attached reference (interpretation)
- The side boughs establish width, lower than the topmost central branches.
- Intermediate branches occupy diagonal upper areas, bridging side and centre. Different heights and depths fill a dome-like crown volume.
- The centre does not form an empty V; inner branches continue toward the top of the rounded envelope.
- The outline is irregular and organic, not a mathematically perfect hemisphere, a flat umbrella or a ring of tips all at the same radius.
- Trunk-to-crown transition is a continuous widening of intertwined bundles, not a cylindrical tube ending at a single common branch plane.
- Dense dark mass, a smaller number of lit bundles, and finer strands at their edges give layered detail. Current models look wire-like because too many equal-thickness, equal-frequency helices are individually exposed.

## Correct next modelling approach (proposal, not yet executed)
1. Establish a tall trunk with a restrained waist and a rounded 3D crown envelope. Review a simple dark silhouette front/side before detailing.
2. Build a shared connected scaffold: low side boughs for width, diagonal intermediate boughs, taller central leaders filling the crown top. Forks must originate on their parent branches.
3. Vary branch junction height/angle/curvature; smoothly taper and preserve continuity through junctions. Do not assign every branch the same outward/upward trajectory.
4. Then route fine black-green strands along this scaffold, splitting bundles at forks. Use irregular winding instead of one common periodic helical cage. Bright strands are selective, not an equally bright separate skeleton.
5. Shape raised roots as branching masses spreading laterally, with local entwining only; avoid radial spider-leg repetition.
6. Only after shape review add lighting/atmosphere/formation animation and plan runtime geometry reduction. Current 65k-triangle weave is a high-detail unbound study, not ready gameplay geometry.

No numeric game size, mechanics, HP or balance has been chosen through this research.
